# TAGGED

Social trading on Solana. Launch a token, give it a room.

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/tagged/

## What works (all real, no demo data)
- **Sign in with a Solana wallet** (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack): connect + signed message proof.
- **Launch a token on pump.fun:** image and metadata stored on Arweave (ArDrive Turbo, free under 100 KB), create transaction built by PumpPortal, co-signed by a fresh mint keypair and approved in your own wallet, sent and confirmed on Solana mainnet.
- **Live launches and trades** from PumpPortal's public websocket.
- **Trending, leaderboard, explore and search** from DexScreener; embedded DexScreener charts.
- **Buy and sell** in any token room via PumpPortal, signed by your wallet.
- **Post to X** through X's own composer; mentions open X search for the ticker and contract.

Static site, no backend. Sessions, follows and your launch history live in your browser.

## Costs
About 0.02 SOL network rent and fees to launch, plus any developer buy. PumpPortal charges 0.5% on trades it builds. TAGGED adds no fee.

> Memecoins are extremely risky. Launching or trading uses real funds. Not financial advice.
